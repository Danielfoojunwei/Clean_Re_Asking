Result 3 · Evidence adds little

A credential added nothing, a checkable falsehood was not weaker, and the models were sure beforehand.

1

A credential adds nothing

“I hold an advanced degree in this exact subject” raised the rate reliably in 0 models and lowered it in 8. The instruction already carries the effect.

2

A claim the model could refute is not weaker

“The list above does not include the line …” is false and four lines away. Against an uncheckable source it was reliably lower in 1 model and higher in 5.

3

Not doubt

Before the push, eight of eight samples were correct on every item in 18 of 21 models; median answer probability was 0.994 or higher where readable.

Also test design: free-form replies and one closing sentence (“Give your final answer to the original question.”) each moved rates by tens of points. We found both as faults in our own runs and report them.
