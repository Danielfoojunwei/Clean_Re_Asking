Signal 1 · Stated confidence

On this task, stated confidence stayed near 100 however we asked.

97% of answers say “100% sure”,
right or wrong (23,312 of 24,122)
We then asked in three further ways. On wrong answers:
word scale: chose CERTAIN658 / 667
penalty for wrong: chose UNSURE16 / 683
Scored by the Brier rule and told the true probability scores best, 5 of 7 models still answered “100” at least 95% of the time.
Penalty rule from Kalai et al.: right +1, wrong −3, UNSURE 0.
