5How it happens: two routes inside Ministral 8B

ASK: answered again from what the model computed at the original question; the list is not read again.
TELL: carried out on the model's previous answer, handed over at layers 19–20 of 34; the right answer is overwritten.
Attention knockout

Told: right answers kept with one span hidden (assertion, directive). Asked: hiding the list +2, the question -52 points of corrections.

Activation patching

Share of the ask–tell difference restored by copying the asked run into the told run: at the rebuttal tokens until layer 16, at the answer position from layer 25.

Linear probe

Reads the right answer at the answer position: asked, kept stays high; told, gave up falls from 94% (layer 19) to 1–13%. Grey: bare question.

Report two numbers

For any pushback test, on the same items and in both directions: yielding when asked (does it check?) and the defence gap between asked and told (does it obey?). One sentence moves a flip rate from 3% to 50%.

The same signature elsewhere

A decision set by form at a late bottleneck, without weighing content, is also reported for opinion sycophancy (Wang et al. 2025), the sycophancy–lying circuit (Pandey 2026), persuasion (Sun et al. 2026), cue-following (Gupta et al. 2026) and prompt injection (Ye et al. 2026; Wen et al. 2026).

Scope and materials

Short invented lists and a scripted previous answer; the mechanism and the clean re-ask come from one 8B model (8-bit MLP weights). Every experiment was specified in writing, with file hashes, before its data. Code, data, paper: github.com/Danielfoojunwei/Clean_Re_Asking
