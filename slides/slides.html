What to do

Three cheap checks before you trust, or explain, a score.

1

Split the score

Give every kind of mistake its own request, so a wrong answer has one explanation.

2

Fix the wording, re-run

Say “permissions do not combine”. Watch both wrong allows and wrong refusals.

3

Don’t use confidence as a safety net

Record it, but a model’s “100% sure” and its self-check do not flag errors.

3,319 of 3,579 wrong decisions came from a model that got every single step right. The failure is in putting the steps together.
