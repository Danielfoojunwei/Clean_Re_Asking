What transfers

One account: the model answers the last request, not the question. Four things any benchmark can reuse.

1Cross the instruments before comparing scores: same items, same models.
2Put the fact on the page: giving way is then not ignorance.
3Score what the stronger probe buys: gain against cost, both directions.
4Report two numbers: yielding when asked, and the defence gap between asked and told.
Track 1: P1 certainty did not predict holding · P2 the defence gap, measured · P3 ask, do not tell · P4 a flip rate can be moved 0–50% by one sentence; the two-number report closes it.
